What problem are we solving?

"I want to invest but I don't know where to start."

01

Low Financial Literacy

Romania ranks among the lowest in the EU. First-time investors must parse 100+ page regulatory PDFs just to understand basic products like TEZAUR.

02

Information is Everywhere

Government bonds, ETFs, mutual funds, stocks, and options are explained in different places, with different terms and rules. No single place brings it all together.

03

No Personalized Guidance

Generic AI can answer general questions, but can't track your spending or remember your goals — and uploading bank statements to ChatGPT raises serious privacy concerns.

Why it matters: People leave money in low-yield bank deposits because navigating alternatives feels impossible.

What Success Looks Like

User success first: from reactive searching to proactive financial decisions.

Before

Users search multiple websites, read dense PDFs, and still feel unsure what to do next.

After

Users ask one question and get clear, actionable guidance in one conversation.

Trust and Privacy

Answers are source-grounded with compliance guidance, and spending insights use anonymized data.

In short: clearer answers, practical next steps, and safer financial decisions.

Why this solution?

An AI agent that knows Romanian finance, remembers you, and protects your data.

LangGraph Supervisor Agent

  • GPT-4o automatically routes to the right tool
  • Searches 13 Romanian financial PDFs via RAG
  • Fetches live market data from the web
  • Creates & tracks savings goals
  • Analyzes bank statements for spending insights

What Makes It Different

  • Hybrid RAG — BM25 + Vector + Cohere Rerank
  • CoALA Memory — remembers your profile & context
  • Privacy-first — transactions categorized locally via Mistral, raw data never leaves your machine
  • Bilingual — works in Romanian and English
  • MiFID II compliant — automatic regulatory disclaimers

2. Market — Live Data

"EUR/RON exchange rate today?" → Agent calls Tavily API, returns live exchange rate with sources.

🎯

"Create a 50,000 RON goal for a car" → Creates savings goal with progress tracking.

🧾

4. Transactions — Insights
